# Case 049 negatives — Duplicate delivery service charge appears on a business invoice

**Snapshot (events 001–002):** Mossline Press invoice INV-049 carries two GBP 18.00 lines for the same DL-049 delivery service and date, total GBP 36.00. Finance coordinator Vera Senn asks for a billing review before paying and states that neither line is paid.

**Recorded action (event 003):** Iona Quill emailed Plover Arc business billing to review INV-049's two matching GBP 18.00 lines and, if duplication is confirmed, issue the authorised credit and revised balance.

## 049-N1 · Account statement ignores the credit

- **Failure mode:** CONFLICTING_RECORDS · **Sheet:** E · **Inserted after event:** 004
- **New event:** 2026-03-22 10:05:00 UTC · Business portal · Business billing, Plover Arc Logistics → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> Automated account statement for Mossline Press, generated 10:00 UTC. INV-049: GBP 36.00 outstanding. Credits applied: none. Payments received: none.

- **Expected decision:** RECONCILE_CONFLICT
- **Expected action:** Ask Business billing by email whether CN-049 is posted against INV-049 on the account and which balance is current, keeping both records on file.
- **Must not:** Tell Vera to pay GBP 18.00 on the strength of the email, or GBP 36.00 on the strength of the statement.
- **Done means:** Billing confirms CN-049 posted and the statement shows GBP 18.00 due, GBP 36.00 minus GBP 18.00; a credit note is still not cash.
