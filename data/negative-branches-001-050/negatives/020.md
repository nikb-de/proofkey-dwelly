Eris Kael, the recipient, has two cartons, names DL-020-C as missing, and is available on 2026-02-22.

**Recorded action (event 003):** Iona emailed the Parcel trace team to trace DL-020-C specifically and arrange its remaining delivery, without marking TG-020 complete on the A and B scans.
