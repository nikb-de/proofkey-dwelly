# Case 029 negatives — Refrigerated food arrives outside the merchant's documented temperature conditions

**Snapshot (events 001–002):** Brineleaf Pantry's documented chilled range for BP-029 / DL-029 is 0 to 5 °C at handover, logger BL-29 recorded 9.2 °C at 08:50 UTC, and only food-safety staff may authorise disposition. Enna Vosk has eaten nothing from the box and asks what to do and whether it will be replaced.

**Recorded action (event 003):** Iona emailed the Brineleaf Pantry food-safety lead asking them to assess BP-029 against the 0 to 5 °C condition using the 9.2 °C reading and to issue a disposition and order-specific remedy, without advising consumption.
