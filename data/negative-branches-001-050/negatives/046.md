# Case 046 negatives — Import broker needs the consignee to appoint it before processing

**Snapshot (events 001–002):** Customs broker Ossmere cannot act on DL-046 until the registered authorised signatory accepts its shipment-specific mandate in the verified business portal. Celand Arc's account administrator supplies a register excerpt naming director Mara Vesk as signatory and states that the operator account cannot sign.

**Recorded action (event 003):** Iona Quill asked Mara Vesk through her verified business-portal channel to complete Ossmere's shipment-specific mandate for DL-046, noting that the broker must confirm acceptance.

- **New event:** 2026-03-19 09:15:00 UTC · Business portal · Account register, business portal → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk
