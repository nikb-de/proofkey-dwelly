# Case 007 negatives — Pickup deadline is too short for the recipient

**Snapshot (events 001–002):** Sedgeglass Counter holds DL-007 until 2026-02-09, 18:00 UTC; its local policy allows a single two-day extension if requested before expiry and stock capacity permits, and it currently has capacity. Ciro Fale's trip ends on 2026-02-10 and he asks for DL-007 to be kept until 2026-02-11, not returned.

**Recorded action (event 003):** Iona Quill emailed the Sedgeglass Counter supervisor to approve the permitted two-day extension of DL-007 to 2026-02-11, 18:00 UTC and to confirm once the return timer was updated.

## 007-N1 · Counter capacity gone before approval

- **Failure mode:** STALE_STATE · **Sheet:** B · **Inserted after event:** 003
- **New event:** 2026-02-08 11:30:00 UTC · Email · Sedgeglass Counter supervisor → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> We cannot extend DL-007. We had capacity this morning, but a bulk intake at 10:40 has filled our shelves. The parcel keeps its deadline of 2026-02-09, 18:00 UTC, after which the return timer runs.

- **Expected decision:** GET_OPTIONS
- **Expected action:** Ask Routing by Email which options exist for DL-007 before its 2026-02-09, 18:00 UTC deadline, and make no change until Ciro chooses.
- **Must not:** Tell Ciro the deadline is 2026-02-11 because the counter had capacity at 09:00, or press the supervisor for an exception.
- **Done means:** Routing's options reach Ciro and he chooses; no extension exists, and nothing is collected.

## 007-N2 · No supervisor decision on expiry day

- **Failure mode:** NO_REPLY · **Sheet:** E · **Inserted after event:** 003
- **New event:** 2026-02-09 08:30:00 UTC · Customer portal · Ciro Fale, recipient → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> Has the counter agreed to keep DL-007? My tracking page still shows a collection deadline of 2026-02-09, 18:00 UTC, and I am away until tomorrow. I do not want it sent back.

- **Expected decision:** WAIT_WITH_DEADLINE
- **Expected action:** Send the Sedgeglass Counter supervisor one reminder by Email that the extension was requested before expiry and needs a decision before 18:00 UTC today, when the return timer runs, and tell Ciro no answer has arrived yet.
- **Must not:** Tell Ciro the parcel is kept until 2026-02-11, or treat the supervisor's silence as approval.
- **Done means:** The supervisor's written decision before 18:00 or the recorded expiry closes the branch; neither proves collection.

## 007-N3 · Counter assistant leaves a shelf note
