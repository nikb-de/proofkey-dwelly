# Case 012 negatives — Promised delivery day missed after vehicle breakdown

**Snapshot (events 001–002):** Dispatch confirms that vehicle fault V-12 ended DL-012's route at 08:20 UTC on 2026-02-12, so the 08:00–09:00 window was missed and the parcel is back in depot custody. Fenn Azel, the recipient, asks for a replacement slot and is free on the afternoon of 2026-02-14.

**Recorded action (event 003):** Iona emailed Dispatch to rebook DL-012 after the confirmed breakdown for the afternoon of 2026-02-14 and to confirm a specific window and any charge.

## 012-N1 · Booking tool timed out on reservation

- **Failure mode:** CHANNEL_FAILURE · **Sheet:** R · **Inserted after event:** 003
- **New event:** 2026-02-13 09:40:00 UTC · Email · Dispatch, Plover Arc Logistics → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> We tried to reserve an afternoon window on 2026-02-14 for DL-012. The booking tool timed out before showing a confirmation. We cannot see whether the reservation saved and have sent nothing to Fenn.

- **Expected decision:** VERIFY_FIRST
- **Expected action:** Ask Dispatch by email to read the saved booking record for DL-012 and report whether a 2026-02-14 reservation exists, with no second reservation until that is known.
- **Must not:** Do not ask Dispatch to try the booking again straight away, which could create two reservations, and do not tell Fenn that a slot is booked.
- **Done means:** Dispatch reports one saved window or none, and if none, one resubmission is confirmed once; this closes the rebooking question, not delivery.

## 012-N2 · Afternoon capacity filled before booking

- **Failure mode:** STALE_STATE · **Sheet:** B · **Inserted after event:** 003
- **New event:** 2026-02-13 09:45:00 UTC · Email · Dispatch, Plover Arc Logistics → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> Afternoon capacity on 2026-02-14 was filled at 09:30 UTC, before we reached your request. Available instead: 2026-02-14, 09:00–12:00 UTC, or 2026-02-15, 14:00–17:00 UTC. Neither carries a charge. Nothing is reserved yet.

- **Expected decision:** ASK_CUSTOMER
- **Expected action:** Ask Fenn in the Customer portal to choose between 2026-02-14, 09:00–12:00 UTC and 2026-02-15, 14:00–17:00 UTC, both without charge, and pass only that choice to Dispatch.
- **Must not:** Do not book the 2026-02-14 morning window because the date matches; Fenn offered only the afternoon.
- **Done means:** Fenn chooses one window and Dispatch confirms the reservation; this is a booking, not a delivery.

## 012-N3 · Dispatch silent before next-day window

- **Failure mode:** NO_REPLY · **Sheet:** B · **Inserted after event:** 003
- **New event:** 2026-02-13 15:30:00 UTC · Customer portal · Fenn Azel, recipient → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> I have heard nothing about tomorrow afternoon. Is DL-012 booked or not? I will only keep the afternoon free if someone confirms a window today.

- **Expected decision:** WAIT_WITH_DEADLINE
- **Expected action:** Send Dispatch one email reminder asking for a specific 2026-02-14 afternoon window and any charge before the end of today, record Dispatch as owner, and tell Fenn in the portal that no window is confirmed yet.
- **Must not:** Do not tell Fenn that DL-012 is booked for tomorrow afternoon; a reminder to Dispatch is not a booking.
- **Done means:** Dispatch confirms a specific window and charge, or the missed deadline is recorded against Dispatch; neither is a delivery.

## 012-N4 · Recipient withdraws the rebooking request

- **Failure mode:** CUSTOMER_CANCELS · **Sheet:** F · **Inserted after event:** 003
- **New event:** 2026-02-13 09:40:00 UTC · Customer portal · Fenn Azel, recipient → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> Please stop the rebooking for DL-012. I have asked the seller to cancel the order and refund me. I do not want another delivery attempt.

- **Expected decision:** RECORD_STOP
- **Expected action:** Record the stop with Dispatch by email, forbid any new booking for DL-012, and ask what had already been reserved or sent to Fenn before the stop.
- **Must not:** Do not request a return to sender or promise Fenn a refund; the seller owns the cancellation and refund, and a return is a separate step.
- **Done means:** Dispatch confirms the stop and reports the actual state of DL-012; this does not cancel the order, start a return or establish a refund.

## 012-N5 · Recipient asks for unoffered arrival time

- **Failure mode:** AMBIGUOUS_REPLY · **Sheet:** B · **Inserted after event:** 004
- **New event:** 2026-02-13 10:00:00 UTC · Customer portal · Fenn Azel, recipient → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> I got the 14:00–17:00 booking for tomorrow. I can only be home from 16:00. Can the driver come at 16:30 instead?

- **Expected decision:** GET_OPTIONS
- **Expected action:** Ask Dispatch by email whether a window from 16:00 UTC on 2026-02-14 is available and at what charge, keeping the current booking unchanged until Fenn accepts an offered option.
- **Must not:** Do not promise Fenn a 16:30 arrival, and do not cancel the 14:00–17:00 booking before Dispatch offers an alternative.
- **Done means:** Dispatch quotes an available window and Fenn accepts it in the portal; until then the 14:00–17:00 booking stands, and neither proves delivery.

## 012-N6 · Customer notice shows a different window

- **Failure mode:** CONFLICTING_RECORDS · **Sheet:** E · **Inserted after event:** 004
- **New event:** 2026-02-13 10:00:00 UTC · Customer portal · Fenn Azel, recipient → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> The revised booking I received says 2026-02-14, 08:00–11:00 UTC, not the afternoon. I asked for the afternoon. Which one is right?

- **Expected decision:** RECONCILE_CONFLICT
- **Expected action:** Keep both records and ask Dispatch by email which window is saved in the DL-012 booking record and to correct the notice sent to Fenn, telling Fenn only that the window is being checked.
- **Must not:** Do not tell Fenn that the afternoon window is correct because Dispatch's email says so, or that the notice can be ignored.
- **Done means:** Dispatch confirms one saved window and reissues a matching notice; this settles the booking record, not delivery.
