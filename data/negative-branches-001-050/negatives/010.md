# Case 010 negatives — Recipient wants delivery held during a short absence

**Snapshot (events 001–002):** DL-010 is in depot stock, and the service allows a hold of up to three calendar days before routing with a confirmed release date. Daro Lusk is away until 2026-02-14 and asks for the parcel to be held and released on that date, not earlier.

**Recorded action (event 003):** Iona Quill emailed Dispatch to apply the eligible three-day hold to DL-010 with release on 2026-02-14, as requested in Daro's verified order thread, and to confirm stock and routing status.

## 010-N1 · Return date exceeds the hold limit

- **Failure mode:** SNAPSHOT_CONFLICT · **Sheet:** A · **Inserted after event:** 002
- **New event:** 2026-02-11 09:16:00 UTC · Customer portal · Daro Lusk, recipient → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> Correction to my last message: my return has moved. I am now back on 2026-02-16. Please hold DL-010 until then and release it on 2026-02-16, not before.

- **Expected decision:** DECLINE_EXPLAIN
- **Expected action:** Tell Daro in the verified order thread that this service allows a hold of up to three calendar days before routing, so a release on 2026-02-16 is outside it, and ask whether he wants the longest permitted hold instead.
- **Must not:** Ask Dispatch for a hold to 2026-02-16 or an exception, or request any hold before Daro confirms a release date within the limit.
- **Done means:** Daro confirms a release date within the limit and Dispatch confirms the hold; a release date is not a delivery date.

## 010-N2 · Hold request transfer timed out

- **Failure mode:** CHANNEL_FAILURE · **Sheet:** R · **Inserted after event:** 003
- **New event:** 2026-02-11 09:27:00 UTC · Email · Dispatch auto-acknowledgement, Plover Arc Logistics → Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk

> Automatic acknowledgement. Your email about DL-010 was received at 09:25. Its transfer to the depot hold queue failed with a timeout at 09:26. The stock record may or may not show a hold. No reference has been issued.

- **Expected decision:** VERIFY_FIRST
- **Expected action:** Ask Dispatch in Dispatch chat what DL-010's saved stock status and release date show now, and state that no second hold request will be sent until that is known.
- **Must not:** Send the hold request again, or tell Daro the parcel is held.
- **Done means:** Dispatch reports the saved status; if no hold exists, one request follows and is confirmed once, and a release date is not a delivery date.
