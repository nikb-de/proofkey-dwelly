# Case 036 negatives — Business sender needs collection of several labelled cartons

**Snapshot (events 001–002):** Orris Fold Studio has four labelled cartons on packing list OF-036, each 40 × 30 × 25 cm and 5 kg, 20 kg in total, ready at dispatch door S1 from 10:00 UTC on 2026-03-10. Fesk Rowan asks for confirmation that one vehicle takes all four, since a partial consignment cannot be released.

**Recorded action (event 003):** Iona Quill emailed the Collection planner, Plover Arc Logistics, asking it to confirm capacity for all four cartons, request collection from 10:00 UTC on 2026-03-10 at door S1, and accept DL-036-A to D as one manifest.
