## Requirements
### Requirement: Public support claims bind to evidence and launch scope
The README SHALL present a concise support table naming each path, evidenced scenario, exact candidate, local evidence link and important limitation. It MUST distinguish recorded installed acceptance from source-only verification, routing from successful workflows, and six offline interfaces from native integrations. Historical J13/J06 candidates and complete attempt denominators MUST remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Engineer chooses an integration path
- **WHEN** an engineer reads the public entrypoint documentation
- **THEN** they can distinguish fixed replay, trusted case preparation, trace projection, registered controls and unsupported integrations without treating one candidate's evidence as another's qualification.

### Requirement: Historical and unfinished support remains explicit
Dated source-discovery bodies SHALL be retained with dated supersession notes. Registered v1 SHALL remain described as run-only. Unfinished stop-v2 SHALL remain in development unless its own completed evidence is available. Trace MUST remain an unverified receipt projection with dispatch unknown, original readback unobserved and current publication files unchecked. Denied, pending, unknown, initiated-return and queued-handoff meanings MUST NOT become success claims.

#### Scenario: New source exists without completed acceptance
- **WHEN** a capability is under implementation or only its syntax is present
- **THEN** the public table labels that boundary and does not claim installed, restart, live-provider or human validation.

### Requirement: Documentation review and closure are independently evidenced
Completion SHALL require local link/anchor and claim checks plus independent review of the exact documentation delta. The change SHALL NOT run or imply new runtime, provider, installation or human acceptance. Only J08 may close from this documentation leaf; combined parent tasks depend on their other leaves.

#### Scenario: Documentation is approved
- **WHEN** exact links and claims have been checked and the independent reviewer approves the docs delta
- **THEN** J08 may close with its own retained report, while J07/J21/J27 and other unfulfilled gates remain open.
