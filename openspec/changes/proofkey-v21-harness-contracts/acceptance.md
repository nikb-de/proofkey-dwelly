# Acceptance and evidence plan

Status: proposed, unexecuted; revised 8 October 2026, Europe/London. Targets come from the user's answer 15. No timed pass, actual provider processing, calibrated labels or protected holdout is claimed. The frozen v2.2 package and its historical 90-minute drill are unchanged.

## Four separate ten-minute targets

Every drill has **one 600-second wall-clock target for its complete declared exercise**, not per case. Freeze packet/workload, required outcomes and entrypoint before starting. For A, freeze the starting baseline and separately retain the resulting human-reviewed release hash; code/profile/process may change only through the declared development loop. B/C/D use a frozen execution candidate throughout. Include declared setup after release, queues, human review/clarification waits, provider latency, retries and final export. Declare preinstalled dependencies and other preparation. Record full finish time; a timeout is unfinished, not a pass. Failure, denied/blocked, partial, missing-output and budget-exhausted cases remain in denominators. No human replacement answers.

| A: new-profile adaptation | One unseen profile packet changing acknowledgement/readback AND process; additional nested/no-text schema change | Packet release → source-grounded proposal, regression, human review and clean hashed release | Both workflows remain correct; no permission expansion; old unfinished intents not reinterpreted; documented unavailable capabilities |
| B: positive resolution | Seven cases: no-text reschedule, free-text whole-order return, transcript reschedule, photo-evidenced whole-order return, PDF-evidenced whole-order return, raw-audio reschedule, completed-service refusal with permitted operator receipt | Frozen batch release → all independent required effects verified and outputs exported | Positive effects for both autonomous families and the required operator route, all six ports used without unnecessary escalation, each required input class genuinely processed with provenance |

The proposed counts are planning workloads, not an estimate of official case count. Freeze exact cases and independent effect-level expectations before measurements; changes start a new version. Untimed regression must also exercise each independent failure driver; a compound rejection is not evidence that every constituent guard worked. A new setup, modality or workload cannot inherit an old receipt. Crash terminal-unknown can be a correct bounded result; it is not completed return creation. An unfamiliar rehearsal packet is not a protected holdout without the custody/isolation gates below.

All three entrypoints require semantic parity tests. A timed run declares which entrypoint it used; it does not establish the other interfaces' speed. Fixture/scripted clarification is explicitly simulated; interactive completion depends on a real authenticated reply and its wall-clock wait counts.

## Labeling and team revalidation

1. Freeze public development/rehearsal cases and deterministic safety/effect invariants. Declare expected-authority assumptions independently of extraction output.
2. LLM-propose labels only through a declared authorized campaign or clearly labeled offline fixtures. Store case/version, exact observed provider/model or unknown identity, prompt/hash, proposed expected facts/actions, evidence references, ambiguity/disagreement and `provisional_llm` status. New paid work needs a budget; this revision runs none.
3. Keep abstentions and disputed labels. Do not force agreement by revealing a preferred outcome. Panel agreement is not proof or calibration.
4. Prepare an **unsent** review pack with case artifacts, proposals, disagreements, evidence and an accept/change/ambiguous rubric. Authorized team reviewers revalidate later; preserve original proposals, reviewer identity/disposition/version and unresolved scope. Approved reviewed cases may become a provisional reviewed reference set, not automatically a protected holdout.
5. Version/hash the label set, rubric and processing lineage; a change invalidates affected cached assessments and requires regrading. Preserve old judgments as historical, not current score evidence. Measure grader repeatability and sensitivity to deliberately improved/degraded candidates before score-based optimization. Retain disagreements/errors and sample sizes. Deterministic forbidden-effect checks are not replaced by labels.
6. Group related variants. Any family used for feedback or selection becomes validation. Protected holdout requires verified isolation and a human custodian who does not tune from hidden data; otherwise report rehearsal only.

## Receipt and stop rules

Receipt fields: run ID/date, starting baseline/resulting release hashes for A or frozen execution candidate for B/C/D, profile/process/policy/prompt/input/label-set/rubric/lineage hashes, processing mode and observed model IDs, interface/commands, declared setup/workload, start/finish elapsed seconds, queue/human/provider time, persisted phase/next gate/question/consent mode/stop/ownership epoch and budgets, per-effect intents/dispatches/readbacks, every case/output/error, unsupported capabilities, forbidden-effect count and reviewer/approval state. Missing evidence remains unknown. No score compression that hides partial effects or denied controls.

Stop dispatch on journal failure, authority/conflict failure, recorded request stop, mandatory-guarantee absence, exhausted deadline/budget or unsafe unknown write. Unknown ownership transfer fences action-bearing decisions. Preserve reconciliation state and attempt only separately permitted bounded status/handoff; no retry or undo follows merely from stop. A handoff failure is visible; it is not completion. Stop a paid campaign at its authorized cap; never switch silently to another paid provider.
