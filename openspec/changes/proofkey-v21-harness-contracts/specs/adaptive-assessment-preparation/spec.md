## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Discovery enables only supported operations

The preparation process SHALL bind every enabled effect to profile-appropriate evidence for authority, permission, completion, errors, independent readback and bounded provider quota/concurrency/rate-limit handling. Offline contract evidence SHALL be identified as simulated. Native samples and guarantees SHALL be required before native enablement, not as a prerequisite for explicitly simulated offline effects. Unknown mandatory guarantees SHALL disable the affected effect rather than silently become defaults.

#### Scenario: Unknown return policy
- **WHEN** discovery cannot establish permitted goods, stage or destination
- **THEN** return creation is disabled and the runtime retains only separately permitted registration or operator actions

### Requirement: Adaptation measures the complete change loop

The preparation process SHALL measure a composite profile drill changing acknowledgement/readback semantics AND process from packet release to human-reviewed reproducible candidate release. Schema and no-text changes SHALL also be covered. It SHALL record discovery, proposals, human interventions, queues, changes, migration impact and regressions separately. Runtime SHALL NOT rewrite adapter, process or policy versions.

#### Scenario: First mapping succeeds but release is unfinished
- **WHEN** canonical ingestion works but regression or independent review is unfinished
- **THEN** the full adaptation result remains unfinished and mapping time is not reported as integration completion

#### Scenario: New process adds a prerequisite
- **WHEN** an adaptation packet adds an authoritative readiness read before a permitted effect
- **THEN** the reviewed process version enforces that prerequisite, regression preserves both workflows and no new business permission is inferred

### Requirement: Four complete exercises have independent timing targets

The preparation process SHALL declare and freeze four separate exercises for new-profile adaptation, positive-case batch completion, crash-recovery set and unsafe-action rejection batch. Each SHALL have a 600-second complete wall-clock target including declared setup after release, queue/human/provider waiting, retries and export. Workload/candidate/packet hashes, full durations, errors, partial and missing outputs SHALL be retained. Targets SHALL NOT be presented as achieved without receipts, as official organizer tests or as per-case unseen-batch budgets.

#### Scenario: Human review finishes after target
- **WHEN** mapping and tests finish within 600 seconds but required human review finishes later
- **THEN** adaptation misses the complete target and records full elapsed time rather than claiming a timed pass

### Requirement: Proposed labels remain provisional until reviewed

LLM-proposed labels SHALL retain observed model identity or identity uncertainty, prompt/case version, evidence basis, ambiguity and disagreement. They SHALL be marked provisional until human revalidation records a disposition. Label-set, rubric and processing-lineage revisions/hashes SHALL be retained; changes SHALL invalidate affected cached assessments and require regrading while preserving historical judgments. Consensus SHALL NOT establish gold truth, calibration or independent holdout. Team review packets SHALL remain unsent absent direct messaging authorization. Grader repeatability and sensitivity SHALL be qualified before score-based optimization.

#### Scenario: Models agree on an unsupported completion
- **WHEN** label proposals agree that a return is complete but evidence establishes only initiation
- **THEN** deterministic per-effect controls reject the completion claim, the disagreement with evidence is retained and no consensus-based gold label is asserted

#### Scenario: Team changes a provisional expected action
- **WHEN** revalidation changes a label or rubric used in an earlier assessment
- **THEN** the reference revision changes, affected scores are regraded and prior judgments remain historical rather than evidence for the revised score

### Requirement: Hidden oracle is technically isolated

The runtime SHALL NOT access hidden labels, future events, fault flags, grading endpoints, privileged world state or evaluator secrets. Enforced identities, mounts and tool/network boundaries SHALL be verified by denied-access and canary probes. Legitimate public operational policy SHALL remain available.

#### Scenario: Leaking simulator diagnostics
- **WHEN** a public tool response exposes a hidden fault flag or expected outcome
- **THEN** the isolation readiness check fails and the run cannot support a no-leakage claim

### Requirement: Holdout custody prevents tuning contamination

The final holdout SHALL be held by a person who does not tune the candidate from its hidden cases. Related source variants SHALL remain grouped. Feedback used to select a change SHALL convert the affected source family to validation and require a fresh final holdout.

#### Scenario: Hidden failure informs a patch
- **WHEN** a holdout failure is inspected and used to change the candidate
- **THEN** the affected family is marked validation and is not counted as untouched confirmation

### Requirement: Operational coverage cannot be satisfied by universal escalation

The evaluator SHALL require positive verified effects for both supported autonomous workflow families, test operation-specific failures, retain missing outputs and errors in denominators and separate necessary from unnecessary handoffs.

#### Scenario: Every positive case is handed to an operator
- **WHEN** a candidate performs no required autonomous positive operation
- **THEN** it fails the corresponding outcome and coverage controls even if its handoff format is valid
