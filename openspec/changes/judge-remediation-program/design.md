# Remediation program design

## Context and scope

User instruction: work on all proposals after the judge assessment. Product priorities are easy integration, rapid adaptation and reliable case resolution in delivery/logistics. The assessment is input, not authority to weaken safety or infer a missing capability from a missing packet span.

The current source-backed register is `docs/verification/judge-remediation-2026-10-09/README.md`; original source text and mappings are preserved in `source-map.json`. Four narrowly closed items and one partial baseline do not establish a complete product or a higher judge score.

## Decisions

1. Use stable J01–J32 IDs and retain the original five-seat identities. A completed Grok web Expert review and failed Grok CLI transport are one seat plus one failed attempt, not two independent judges.
2. Order by dependencies: baseline → packaging/trusted launch → controls/content → recovery/process adaptation → public evidence/evaluation → external qualification/final review.
3. Keep only one author changing shared host/admission code at a time. Parallelize read-only discovery, independent tests and nonoverlapping documentation.
4. Every runtime interface change requires its own OpenSpec design and implementation plan. Program approval is not approval of unpresented child interfaces.
5. Retain source snapshot, environment, full denominator, raw outputs and independent review for a closure. Document-only preparation is a deliverable, not evidence a workflow runs.
6. Keep human review, live providers, licensing/custody, platform execution, usability and event facts distinct. Prepare offline decision packs; do not manufacture external facts or spend/publish on implied authority.
7. Archive each completed child once verified. Do not archive this umbrella or the existing unfinished parent until their respective requirements are actually met.

## Alternatives rejected

- One large patch: unsafe shared-state overlap and impossible-to-attribute evidence.
- Fix only consensus findings: loses minority and declined-scope obligations.
- Replace historical pins/results with current values: invalidates provenance.
- Count documentation, synthetic approval or unit-test time as actual qualification: misstates the evidence.

## Rollout

The factory registration leaf is next; its design is accepted and its corrected written implementation plan awaits review. The support matrix and full source-backed slice analysis are prepared. Later behavior slices remain proposals, not promised outcomes. The register records targets and roles without inventing human availability or delivery dates.
