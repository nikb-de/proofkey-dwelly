# Design

Use a 450×800 portrait composition with a 344×748 dark device shell, rounded clipped screen, sensor island, status bar and home indicator. These are presentation choices, not claims of an exact hardware model. Keep essential content inside safe areas. Use 44×44 minimum interactive targets, a clear primary CTA and persistent destination navigation. Do not use the tab bar as a disguised sequence of replay steps.

The five moments are request, model/operator review, changed terms, consent replay and local booking receipt. Shorten framing copy, not authoritative terms. Preserve provenance and never imply live execution or completed physical delivery.

Sources: [Apple Layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout), [Buttons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/buttons), [Tab bars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars), [Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography).

Record both modes concurrently using existing sanitized bundles. The shared capture contract supplies eight-second timing, the 40-second total and 1,200-frame export expectation. End-anchor the cut to avoid the browser wall-clock/video time-origin mismatch discovered previously. No fresh provider calls or case mutations are allowed.
