# Mobile case recordings

User request: record both scripted and live-LLM variants of the original case-001 terms negotiation in 50-second mobile format.

Implemented a bounded capture runner, read-only final-state pickup and deterministic video assembler. No production API or operational state-machine change. Traceable requirements are archived into `openspec/specs/mobile-case-recording/spec.md`; artifact receipts and semantic limitations are in `docs/demos/2026-10-10-case-001-terms/RECORDINGS.md`.
