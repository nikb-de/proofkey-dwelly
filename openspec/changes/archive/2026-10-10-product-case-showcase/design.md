# Design

Use a dedicated `/showcase.html` route on the existing platform server, with explicit static assets. The UI reads a compact generated bundle; it cannot POST, invoke models, or change cases. Each bundle retains the originating case ID, source hash, source event IDs, historical model identity and sandbox receipt.

Right onTime Forest/Paper palette, the existing wordmark and local Manrope/Inter fonts. One moment per viewport, legible mobile layout, subtle transitions, prominent date/time and human-readable terms. No raw JSON in primary content. An always-visible replay/sandbox label and a record-details drawer establish the boundary without overwhelming the story.

LLM descriptions remain verbatim and advisory. A recorded operator action is not attributed to the model when they differ. Only the final scene shows the final booking receipt; never imply physical delivery. Customer acceptance controls advance a replay; they do not create authenticated consent.

Capture both variants at a mobile viewport. The film may compress elapsed time, but the player labels the content as recorded replay, not a live autonomous run.
