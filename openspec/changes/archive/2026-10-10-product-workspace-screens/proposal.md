# Product workspace screens

## Why
The integrated backend is exposed through an operator workbench, while the approved Right onTime design describes a Team workspace, customer journey, requests and preparation. Bring those product surfaces onto the existing backend without replacing its durable records or safety gates.

## What changes
- Team home, searchable cases, meaningful status counters, Summary / Requests / Sources and visible next actions.
- A case-bound, operator-assisted customer journey for proposing a delivery window and reading its actual review/execution status.
- A preparation workspace showing real capabilities, a local integration-profile draft and explicit preflight results.
- Static routes and browser verification for all screens.

## Impact
Frontend and static serving only. No schema migration, authenticated customer portal, external messaging, arbitrary profile activation, automatic execution or paid model calls are introduced.
