## Why

The controlled synthetic factory cannot yet qualify an executable process change: its existing source registration does not authorize a changed process, and the window workflow has no separately observed readiness step or raw scheduling envelope. A finite window-only adaptation will make those contracts testable while preserving original identities and reporting the limits of historical-state evidence.

## What Changes

- Add the run-only trusted factory `fixed-synthetic-window-readiness-v3`, candidate `flat-window-readiness-v1` (profile family `v1.3`), and window process `reschedule-window-readiness-v2` / `v2-window-readiness`.
- Insert one readiness phase with four separately leased source reads and an explicit synthetic `scheduling.slot == S0` prerequisite. Keep the 24-call, 120-second active and 10-second tool limits.
- Add actual private raw scheduling source envelopes, strict bounded adapter decoding, and window-only original terminal proof using the full original join. Acknowledgement and HTTP 200 alone never establish success or rejection.
- **BREAKING for the opt-in candidate's accepted scheduling-read domain:** candidate scheduling reads, including exact-slot consumers, reject legacy-valid responses outside the new raw envelope bounds. Exact-slot dispatch remains typed. No fallback, truncation, resend or loss of existing effects follows from decoder failure; legacy profiles keep their typed domain.
- Add exact trusted semantic registration, independently fixed full-package source pins, proposal/manifest commitments and narrowly enumerated profile changes. Preserve legacy registration modes and historical hashes.
- Add the explicit candidate profile resource and mirror to package lists and trusted provisioning. Preserve default v1 and completed stop-v2 behavior; v3 does not inherit v2 stop authority.
- Separate current-source behavioral qualification from historical pending-state preservation. The discovered historical J06 pair is completed-only; it cannot pass pending preservation. J05 remains open for unmet broader evidence.
