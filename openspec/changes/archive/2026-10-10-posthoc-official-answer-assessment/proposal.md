# Assess the retained official answers

## Why

The published 30 official answers have not received per-case multi-provider
assessment. The separate development reliability pilot is not that assessment.

## What Changes

Add an explicitly post-hoc, reference-free assessment of the exact retained
answers using three current API judges and the existing eight rubric anchors.
Preserve original answer/source bytes, all request/response traces and failures.
Do not change the operational system or its official answers.

## Impact

Only an auxiliary assessment report, reproducible review tooling and audit
artifacts are added. Judge opinions are not organiser grades, validated human
ground truth or authorization for any business action.
